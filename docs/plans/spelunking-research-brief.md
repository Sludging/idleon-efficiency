## 1. What the mechanic does

Spelunking combines character gathering with account progression. Characters gather Spelunky Pages and skill EXP in World 7 caves. Players read pages to improve the shared Lore Journal, and delve through tunnels to break objects, collect Amber, find discoveries, and reach Lore bosses. Gathering and delving are distinct parts of the same feature. [P1, G1–G3]

Gathering efficiency is not delve POW. The game has separate helpers for gathering efficiency, speed, EXP, and page yield, and for delve POW, rock requirements, HP, and Amber. Their full calculations depend on many account systems. The approved website scope explains these terms and mechanics; it does not calculate personalized efficiency or expected run results. [G1, G2]
