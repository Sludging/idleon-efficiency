# Menagerie: product specification draft

**Status:** Historical product-scope proposal. Part 1 completed and human-accepted — 2026-10-18; #467 is closed as completed. See the [current handoff](menagerie-research-brief.md#current-handoff) for accepted implementation and verification limits. Part 2 remains proposed and unselected. This draft records original scope, not current binding design. **Tracking:** [Menagerie feature issue #466](https://github.com/Sludging/idleon-efficiency/issues/466); [closed Part 1 delivery issue #467](https://github.com/Sludging/idleon-efficiency/issues/467).

**Scope source:** [Menagerie research brief](menagerie-research-brief.md). **Conditional delivery proposal:** [Menagerie delivery parts](menagerie-delivery-parts-draft.md).

## Problem

Players lack a trustworthy website view of Menagerie progression. They need to understand current mission progress, saved Shell/Lumi state, unlocks and purchased bonuses, and what all six side jellyfish currently contribute. Imported account values must not look like live game state, and potential rewards must not look owned or active.

## Solution

Provide a read-only, account-based Menagerie experience using verified in-game names, descriptions, and tooltips only. Show current account-stat mission progress separately from saved completion count and the next unclaimed requirement. Distinguish locked missions from available missions; being over a threshold never claims a mission or grants Shells. Distinguish Shells, Lumi balance, Lumi rank, unlock availability/ownership, purchased levels, affordability, and active effects. Include all six named side panels and supported saved progression, readiness, and current values. Apply verified effects once only to existing modeled recipient calculations. Do not provide game actions or purchase recommendations.

## User stories

1. **Mission progress and claims** — Show the persistent available-mission count with a tooltip describing verified contributors and where more missions come from. In the mission list, show available and locked distinctly. For each mission, derive the first unclaimed task from its saved claim count; show that requirement separately from current account progress and later thresholds already met. Only an available mission meeting its current task is Ready. Progress alone never advances the claim count, awards Shells, or makes a locked mission Ready. Do not hardcode 25 or 33 available missions, or treat seven visible rows as an active limit.

2. **Shells, rank, and Lumi** — As a player, I want to see saved Shell count, Lumi rank, saved Lumi balance, and calculated Lumi per hour, so I can understand my progression and income. Keep the Shell threshold distinct from rank-up readiness until the actual rank transition is verified. Do not treat imported balance as live or extrapolate offline gains without evidence.

3. **Unlocks and bonus levels** — Show one-time unlocks separately from repeatable bonus levels. Use verified in-game identity and descriptions only. Locked bonuses show their verified source description/art and required rank or unlock condition, without current benefit, next-level comparison, or next-cost/affordability fields. Available bonuses, including available level-zero bonuses, show current level/effect and next effect/cost/affordability when valid. Omit next-level fields at a source-confirmed cap. Show currently usable resource balances; retain ownership/consumption distinctions internally, not as default historical display.

4. **Verified effects in existing calculations** — As a player, I want verified in-game descriptions and current effect values for Menagerie bonuses and side panels, with verified effects applied once to existing IE calculations such as cooking speed or research EXP. Integrate only recipients already modeled; do not assume new recipient models or structural extensions. Purple/Yellow input and formula validation still gate accurate Lumi/hour; the supplied catalog has no confirmed new upstream static blocker. See the [side-jelly evidence note](../research/menagerie-jelly-evidence.md).

5. **Six side jellyfish** — As a player, I want to inspect Purple, Blue, Red, Silver, Yellow, and Green panels, their unlock status and supported saved progression, so I can understand each panel without confusing unlock with ownership or affordability. Show Purple's coin-funded purchase levels and Lumi-generation effect; Blue's saved Ladle donation and daily cooking contribution; Red's stored reading opportunity and readiness only while it is positive; Silver's two Obol Fragment upgrades and caps; Yellow's generated progress, four purchased levels, and supported effects; and Green's Gallery requirement and reward identity. Do not infer Green's claimed/owned state from meeting its threshold.

6. **Imported account context** — As a player, I want Menagerie values presented as information from my loaded account, with calculated values clearly separated from saved values, so I can judge what the page actually knows. Do not label imported data as live game state or imply a claim, purchase, donation, or reading action occurred on the website.

## Product decisions

- Deliver two substantial end-to-end outcomes, each spanning trusted account information through usable UI: **Part 1 — Understand Menagerie progression and Lumi**, covering missions, Shell/rank/Lumi, unlocks, 25 bonus levels/effects, and verified effects applied once to existing calculations; **Part 2 — Understand all six side jellyfish**, covering all six panels, source-backed descriptions/current values, and verified effects. Part 1 is selected and approved for continued workflow ([issue #467](https://github.com/Sludging/idleon-efficiency/issues/467)); Part 2 remains unselected.
- Part 2 depends on Part 1's shared account/page/access/unlock/Lumi context. The supplied jellyfish catalog and models align with known static needs; no new upstream static blocker is confirmed. IE owns save semantics, dynamic formulas, cost/effect derivation, readiness, ordering, and native parity. Catalog labels are not established as verbatim in-game copy. No deeper Part 2 investigation was performed; see the [side-jelly evidence note](../research/menagerie-jelly-evidence.md).
- Apply verified effects only to existing modeled recipient calculations, once. Examples include cooking speed or research EXP where those recipients are already modeled. Side-panel behaviors include Purple Lumi generation, Blue daily cooking contribution, Red Spelunking reading, Silver Obol MISC and automatic fragment conversion, Yellow Construction/building and Lumi effects, and Green's Gallery-gated cosmetic reward. These descriptions do not promise new recipient models or structural extensions.
- Unlock ownership or description does not include building its recipient feature. Structural extensions such as Verisimilitude artifact tiers, Coral Kid Singularity, Crucible, Nightmariners, and similar systems are excluded.
- Use verified in-game names, descriptions, and tooltip text only; do not invent identity, titles, explanatory prose, or expanded mechanics. Functional interface labels such as “Level,” “Current task,” and “Available” are permitted.
- Keep available mission count in persistent summary with tooltip for verified contributors and where more missions come from. Show each mission's first unclaimed task separately from current account progress and later thresholds met; only available plus current-task met is Ready.
- Show state distinctions: saved mission claims versus current account-stat progress; first unclaimed task versus later thresholds met; locked versus available; saved values versus calculated effects; imported snapshot versus live state; access/availability versus ownership; ownership versus affordability; purchased levels versus potential benefits; and currently usable resources versus consumed/owned history.
- Use contextual bonus rows: locked entries show verified description/art and required rank/unlock condition only; available entries show level/current effect and valid next effect/cost/affordability; source-confirmed maxed entries omit next fields. See [delivery structure](menagerie-delivery-parts-draft.md#proposed-part-1-page-structure).
- Browsing and details work without hover. Exact layout, navigation placement, and visual design remain for later authorized UI planning.

## Verification approach

- Use repository account-processing seams and domain public outputs with matching-version game-derived expected values. Cover meaningful saved-state and calculation boundaries, including mission availability/progress/completions, Shell/rank limits, Lumi inputs/rate, unlock ownership and bonus levels, affordability, and resource ownership/use. Do not test implementation wiring or copied static catalogs as a substitute for behavior.
- Follow repository offline-first game-access policy. Verify readiness, source, and matching version before any permitted runtime query; use native source and verified extracted values as evidence. Personalized Menagerie runtime parity is not established: prior runtime function/state queries failed with HTTP 500.
- For later authorized UI work, exercise the actual route with imported account state and assert visible player outcomes, including distinct saved/calculated, locked/owned, and imported/live semantics. Preview data proves layout only. No UI verification is claimed by this draft.
- Resolve mission count discrepancy, initial access condition, exact rank-up transition, complete save mapping, Lumi offline behavior, and uncertain readiness/ownership semantics before asserting affected outcomes. Current catalog comparison confirms no new upstream static blocker; seek extraction only if later evidence proves a fixed input is missing. Approval does not waive these evidence gates.

## Out of scope

Optimization rankings, purchase ordering, time-to-goal simulation, gameplay controls or website claim/purchase/donation/reading actions, Liquid Shop rebuild, exact visual or navigation design, and unrelated v1.23 fixes. Structural recipient systems remain out of scope even when a Menagerie unlock names or describes them. Do not hardcode mission availability or missing side-jelly static values.

## Evidence and approval

This draft follows the source boundaries and unresolved questions in the [research brief](menagerie-research-brief.md) and linked core, side-jelly, and site research notes. Matching v1.23 local source supports the main loop; no successful personalized runtime output parity exists. The [delivery-parts draft](menagerie-delivery-parts-draft.md) describes proposed outcomes and dependencies. Part 1 is selected and approved; its information structure may be revisited against a rendered page. The backend plan is approved, but implementation must first obtain paired matching-version native outputs and a real saved account. TDD must use those expected values and domain public outputs. Part 1 UI planning and Part 2 rendered design remain unapproved.
