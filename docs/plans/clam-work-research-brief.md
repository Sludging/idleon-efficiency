## Mechanic and account state

Clamworks is a World 7 active combat activity. Noble Clams drop normal and pure pearls into a **shared account pearl balance**. Players can AFK there, but the player-facing Wiki says AFK earnings are one-fifth of active earnings. Native map setup loads the Clam Work panel; the account save owns the pearl/class progression. [Wiki: The Clamworks](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/The_Clamworks) · native v1.23 `idleon1.23.txt:279528–279537, 189647–189657, 281357–281385`.
