# Spelunking Part 3: backend delivery plan

## Outcomes

Deliver backend data for [#437 — Amber, equipment, and advanced progression](https://github.com/Sludging/idleon-efficiency/issues/437):

- Show account Amber and shop upgrades with saved levels, caps, unlock requirements, and intrinsic effects. Show next cost and affordability only when all source-mapped inputs and current-price behavior are verified.
- Show the seven `SpelunkingToolRepo` identities, base stamina costs, hit radii, damage multipliers, unlock kinds, and automatic-use setting/gates. Do not claim a current selected tool, team, or active AUTO run: those selections are actor-local, not saved account state.
- Show indexed elixir descriptions alongside owned count, saved memorized IDs, equipped slots, and limits. Do not infer names or structured effects from descriptions.
- Show read-only Manic, Overstim, and Trackometer saved progression, gates, thresholds, and effects. Do not simulate refill overflow or mode progression.

This plan extends existing Spelunking data. Preserve accepted Part 1 Amber-haul denomination/formatting and Part 2 stamina/recharge/Trackometer maximum-stamina behavior. Do not add authored player-facing descriptions, an efficiency calculator, or an independent Amber/shop owner.

## Responsibilities

Keep `Spelunking` in `data/domain/world-7/spelunking.tsx` as account coordinator, repository initializer, raw-save boundary, and integration owner. Follow MODEL-01: each concept parses and interprets its own fields; the parent delegates rather than bulk-parsing every feature. Pass source save values only to the owning concept parser, which immediately translates them into named state. Extend existing concepts and add only small concepts justified by distinct state/behavior. Do not create a general aspect framework.

- **Upgrade:** Extend existing `Upgrade` to own its saved level, cap, gates, bonus, cost metadata, and intrinsic description. Delegate each saved level from the parent parser. Reuse `SpelunkingUpgradeRepo` directly; its generated records already provide names, descriptions, costs, caps, effects, and upgrade requirements.
- **Tools and automatic use:** Use `SpelunkingToolRepo` for its seven identities, stats, and default/upgrade/unavailable unlock states. Do not author descriptions or infer saved current tool/team selection. Show automatic full-clear setting from `OptionsListAccount[538]` separately from eligibility: AUTO requires upgrade 12 or gem purchase 43 and account option `[478] >= 15`; actor-local AUTO activity/speed is not saved setting. Team-selection capacity is outside this backend outcome.
- **Elixir:** Expose indexed `SpelunkingElixirRepo` descriptions unchanged beside saved ownership, memorized IDs, equipped slots, and limits. Preserve equipped `[7][0..4]` (`-1` means empty) and separate memorized IDs in `[48]`. Derive owned count `min(12, ShopUpgBonus(23))`, equipped slots `min(5, 1 + ShopUpgBonus(24))`, duplicate cap `min(5, ShopUpgBonus(25))`, and memorization slots from rounded `ShopUpgBonus(57)`. Expose consumption reduction `min(0.6, (ShopUpgBonus(26) + ChapterBonus(3,2)) / 100)` from prepared inputs. Do not infer equipped, memorized, or active state from ownership; active effects are not save-backed.
- **Manic:** Own saved mode toggle `[4][10]`, per-tunnel depth records `[47]`, unlock from upgrade 53, 30x stamina-cost rule, and effects of upgrades 54–56 scaled by Manic depths. Read `[47]` as saved progression; unobserved writer timing does not block parsing.
- **Overstim and Trackometer:** Read-only progression concepts own Overstim level `[4][1]`, fill `[4][2]`, unlock from upgrade 6, threshold `100 * 1.3^level`, and Amber effects. Bonus per level is `30 + ShopUpgBonus(6)`; Amber gain multiplies by `1 + ShopUpgBonus(6) * OverstimBonus / 100`. Explain source-backed refill overflow as context only; do not simulate it, cap stamina, consume thresholds, or increment levels. Trackometer owns level `[4][8]`, fill `[4][9]`, threshold `1000 * 1.5^level`, unlock `ShopUpgBonus(59,1) >= 1`, and effects 59–61 scaled by level. Do not consume thresholds or increment levels.

## Required data and prerequisites

Use `SpelunkingUpgradeRepo`, `SpelunkingToolRepo`, `SpelunkingElixirRepo`, and `SpelunkingChapterRepo` directly. Upgrade records provide names, descriptions, costs, caps, effects, and requirements. Tool records provide seven indexed names, stats, and explicit unlock kinds. Elixir records provide twelve indexed intrinsic descriptions only; pass them through unchanged and do not infer missing names or structured effect data. Do not copy static values or edit generated directories.

Current-price mapping research found existing producer interfaces and order, but not verified numerical parity. The v1.22 source uses Talent 235 × `CalcTalentMAP[235]`; meal 68 `SplkUpg` scaled by the first saved player's Spelunking level in floored 50-level bands (maximum two); `max(Sushi 6, Sushi 27) + Jelly 22` with at least 10% retained; tier factors `9.5^column` and `6.3^row`; and extra `10^7`/`10^9` factors at indices 52+/66+. Website candidates are `Player.getTalentBonusAtInputs` / account talent calculations, `Cooking.getMealBonusForKey("SplkUpg")`, `Sushi.getBonusFromIndex(6|27)`, Jelly obstruction bonus index 22, parsed shop levels, and `players[0]`'s Spelunking skill level. Pipeline updates Jelly, Cooking, Sushi, players, and talent contexts before late Spelunking presentation. Talent 235 equivalence and full producer-to-source numerical mapping remain unverified. POST `/exec` with `{"expression":"1+1"}` returned HTTP 500, so no representative source-versus-website price output was accessible. Expose generated static cost facts; omit dynamic price and affordability until input mapping and source-output comparison verify a case.

Keep shop pricing within existing `Upgrade`/`Spelunking` ownership. `Upgrade` owns each record's price interpretation; `Spelunking` prepares shared inputs once from producer APIs after their values settle. Use saved username order for first-player skill level, never the highest or an arbitrary character. Do not calculate cross-domain values during parsing or duplicate producer bonus logic. Current price fields remain omitted until source-mapped inputs and representative outputs are verified. Overstim remains read-only; Chapter Two overflow contribution is context, not a reason to simulate overflow.
