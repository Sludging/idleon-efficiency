## 1. What The Hole does

The loop is: collect resources and Opals, allocate Opals to villager growth, gain levels and unlocks, spend competing resources on improvements, then gain better production and rewards. Active challenges, passive production, character skilling, and account upgrades are different parts of that loop. [P1, W1, G1, G3]

**Confirmed baseline:** A player can understand the entire feature's current progression and requirements, distinguish owned from possible benefits, inspect verified capability, stats and next costs, see resource competition, and recognize shared limits.
