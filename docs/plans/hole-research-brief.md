## 1. What The Hole does

The Hole is entered from the right side of World 5's Mister Brightside map. Its campfire opens villager features. Polonai opens deeper caverns; other villagers are rescued within them. The loop is: collect resources and Opals, allocate Opals to villager growth, gain levels and unlocks, spend competing resources on improvements, then gain better production and rewards. Active challenges, passive production, character skilling, and account upgrades are different parts of that loop. [P1, W1, G1, G3]

Current scope is **18 released caverns across four biome groups**. `CavernsOwned` returns `min(18, PolonaiLevel)`. Publisher releases corroborate Fountain/Trench as 16/17 and Crystal Glunko Cove as 18. Extra source slots, a fourth monument, Elder/unknown villagers, and future reward descriptions do not establish released content. Historical launch had ten caverns/four villagers. Historical Wisdom news calls it cavern 12; current ordering is **Evertree 12, Wisdom 13**. [G1, G18, W1, W4, P1–P3]

### Ownership and meaning

Most progression lives in account-wide `Holes`, alongside separate `Jars` and account options. However, `Holes[0][characterIndex]` records each character's cavern assignment. Character skill efficiency, combat, inventory, and AFK inputs remain character-specific. A shared cavern progress total does not identify each character's contribution or current activity. [G3, G5, G6, G13, G16, O1]

| Distinction | Meaning for website information |
|---|---|
| Saved progression versus possible reward | A built flag, purchased level, earned Opal, or claimed milestone is owned progression. A static description or unlocked reward is not proof it was earned. [G3, G7, G15] |
| Stored progress versus claimed level | Villager EXP and Study progress can exceed their next requirement before camp UI converts them into levels. Show saved level and claim-ready progress separately. [G3, G5] |
| Stock versus work | Sediment, notes, Rupies, wishes, torches, shapes, and Fountain wallet balances are spendable stocks. Skilling materials gathered toward a layer are dedicated cavern progress, not ordinary inventory stacks. [G6, G9, G10, G13, G16, G17] |
| Saved settings versus actor selection | Harp layout/tune, Bell mode, Study target, and boosted Cove shapes have saved settings. Selected UI tabs, highlighted upgrades, reset target, and active minigame choices are actor-only state. [G3, G9, G10, G12, G16] |

Hypothetical allocations, manual court flags, simulated layouts, or website-selected actors are user inputs, not discovered save fields. Browsing a character row must not imply the website changed that character's game assignment.
