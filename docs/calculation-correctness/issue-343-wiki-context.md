# Issue 343 — official IdleOn Wiki terminology context

Scope: terminology only. The official IdleOn Wiki is contextual evidence; repository implementation, extracted game-code results, and live-game behavior remain authoritative for any formula or internal parameter mapping. Pages/searches below were checked on 2026-08-21.

## Findings

- **Feature name and player-facing meaning:** The official Wiki calls the feature **Cooking Mastery**. Its [Rift Bonuses](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/The_Rift#Rift_Bonuses) table says it unlocks at Rift level 61; the World 4 meal menu then has a **MASTERY** shelf, where players gain Mastery EXP to rank up. Each rank gives pink and yellow points: pink points are spent boosting cooking in various ways, while yellow points boost meals directly; the entry also says the maximum Ribbon Rank is raised. This establishes the player-facing context relevant to `CookMaster`, but does not identify an internal save array or calculation.

- **Cooking meals and meal bonuses:** [Cooking](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cooking) describes Cooking as a World 4 skill that creates Meals with permanent buffs; meals are cooked passively and upgraded with additional quantities of the same meal. The page's [Improving Meal Bonuses](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cooking#Improving_Meal_Bonuses) section uses “meal bonuses” for effects that can be strengthened by the Black Diamond Rhinestone console jewel, shiny pets, Summoning battle wins, and ribbons. The [Bonuses](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cooking#Bonuses) tables separately label **Meal Bonuses** and **Cooking Speed**, including player-facing terms such as “x All Meal Bonuses,” “x Meal Bonuses,” “Meal Cooking Speed,” “Multi Meal Cooking Speed,” and “Cook SPD multi.” These terms provide context only; the Wiki does not say which one, if any, is the meaning of `BonusMultiCook`.

- **Do not conflate meal levels with Cooking Mastery:** [Leveling Meals](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cooking#Leveling_Meals) says meal levels are increased with cooked meals and that leveling meals supplies cooking-speed bonuses through Diamond Chef, Fluoride, and Blood Marrow. That is separate player-facing terminology from the [Cooking Mastery](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/The_Rift#Rift_Bonuses) shelf and does not establish that `CookMaster` means a meal level, cooking speed, or a displayed meal-bonus multiplier.

- **Internal labels are not Wiki terminology:** The official Wiki API's exact-term searches for [`CookMaster`](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=%22CookMaster%22&format=json) and [`BonusMultiCook`](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=%22BonusMultiCook%22&format=json) returned no results. **Unresolved:** the Wiki does not define the repository/game labels `CookMaster` or `BonusMultiCook`, their meal-index semantics, or their formula. Those mappings must come from repository/game-code and live-game evidence.

## Bottom line

The official Wiki's relevant feature name is **Cooking Mastery**: a Rift 61 unlock that adds a World 4 meal-menu Mastery shelf, ranks earned with Mastery EXP, pink points for cooking boosts, yellow points for direct meal boosts, and a Ribbon Rank-cap increase ([The Rift](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/The_Rift#Rift_Bonuses)). The Wiki also uses **meal bonuses** and distinct cooking-speed terms ([Cooking](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cooking#Bonuses)), but it does not resolve `CookMaster` or `BonusMultiCook` into any of those terms or define their formula. Use the repository implementation plus extracted/live-game evidence as the formula authority.
