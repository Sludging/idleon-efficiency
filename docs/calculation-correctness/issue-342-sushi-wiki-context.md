# Issue 342 — official IdleOn Wiki terminology context

Scope: terminology only. The official IdleOn Wiki and its first-party MediaWiki API are contextual evidence; repository implementation, extracted game-code results, and live-game behavior remain authoritative for any formula or internal parameter mapping. Pages/searches below were checked on 2026-08-21.

## Findings

- **Feature name and player-facing screens:** The official Wiki calls the feature **Sushi Station**. Its page describes three screens: **Sushi**, where players cook and merge sushi to reach higher tiers; **Upgrades**, where Bucks improve Fuel, unlock mechanics, and improve station gains; and **Bonus**, which shows Knowledge levels and the bonuses provided by sushi ([Sushi Station](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Sushi_Station)). The same page defines a **Knowledge Bonus** as a bonus applied to each Sushi based on its Knowledge level. It says Sushi Knowledge contributes to global effects such as Bucks generation, Knowledge XP, and fuel efficiency, and that **Seared Knowledge** increases the effectiveness of all Sushi Knowledge bonuses. This establishes the station's player-facing terminology but does not expose a save array, index, or calculation label.

- **Closest exact player-facing Prisma wording:** In the Sushi Station table, **Tier 24 — Tobo Tamago** has the bonus **“All your Prisma Bubbles give a 0.01x higher bonus than before.”** The same row lists the separate Knowledge Bonus as **“Faster Fuel Generation”** with **3/level** as its base value ([Sushi Station](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Sushi_Station)). The official Sushi changelog also records Tobo Tamago and the same Prisma Bubble bonus wording ([Changelog/1.10/Sushi](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Changelog/1.10/Sushi)). Thus, the Wiki's most specific player-facing description relevant to `PrismaBonusMult` is a higher bonus for all Prisma Bubbles, not a generic Sushi Station fuel or Bucks bonus.

- **Prisma bonus terminology elsewhere:** The official Pets page describes the Rift Hivemind companion as providing **“+50% Prisma Bubble bonus multi and +5 Prisma Bubbles”** ([Pets](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Pets)). This confirms that the Wiki uses “Prisma Bubble bonus” and “bonus multi” as player-facing terminology, but it does not say that either phrase is the internal `PrismaBonusMult` label or define the arithmetic. The general [Alchemy](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Alchemy) page explains that bubbles can be passive or active, but it likewise does not define the requested internal parameter.

- **Research terminology and Sushi Station access:** The official [Research](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Research) page describes Research as unlocking powerful bonuses. Its grid includes **Sushi Station Linguistics**, which lets the player speak to the knife past Glimbo to play the Sushi Station game and gives **(0.25 per level)x more Bucks gained from your Sushi**. The neighboring **Shakin' It** square grants extra daily shaker uses and a chance of free shaker use at the Sushi Station. These are explicitly Research/Sushi Station bonuses and should not be conflated with the Tier 24 Prisma Bubble bonus.

- **Exact-term API searches:** The official Wiki API returned no results for [`"RoG_BonusQTY"`](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=%22RoG_BonusQTY%22&format=json), [`"SushiStuff"`](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=%22SushiStuff%22&format=json), or [`"PrismaBonusMult"`](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=%22PrismaBonusMult%22&format=json). Searching the exact player-facing phrase [`"All your Prisma Bubbles give a 0.01x higher bonus"`](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=%22All%20your%20Prisma%20Bubbles%20give%20a%200.01x%20higher%20bonus%22&format=json) returned **Sushi Station** and **Changelog/1.10/Sushi**. Searching [`"Prisma Bubble bonus multi"`](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=%22Prisma%20Bubble%20bonus%20multi%22&format=json) returned **Pets** and a companion changelog. The Sushi Station revision metadata API records revision 93677 at 2026-08-20T19:13:05Z ([revision query](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&prop=revisions&titles=Sushi%20Station&rvprop=ids%7Ctimestamp&rvlimit=1&format=json)).

## Bottom line

The official Wiki identifies the relevant player-facing concept as a **Prisma Bubble bonus**: Tier 24 Tobo Tamago is documented as making **all Prisma Bubbles give a 0.01x higher bonus**, while other pages use the related phrase **Prisma Bubble bonus multi**. However, the Wiki and first-party API do not identify `RoG_BonusQTY(23)` or prove that it is Tobo Tamago. The safe canonical issue title/name is **“Sushi Station: identify the player-facing Prisma Bubble bonus for `RoG_BonusQTY`”**; avoid naming the internal parameter as Tobo Tamago until game-code/live extraction confirms the mapping.
