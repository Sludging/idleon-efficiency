# Issue 354 — official IdleOn Wiki terminology context

Scope: terminology only. The official IdleOn Wiki is contextual evidence; repository extraction results and live-game behavior remain authoritative for any formula or parameter mapping. Pages/searches below were checked on 2026-08-21.

## Findings

- **The Button vs. Minehead:** [The Button](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/The_Button) describes a giant green button in Trench Precipice that grants account bonuses when its press requirements are met; its press table includes bonuses such as Minehead Currency, Cooking SPD, and Crop Evo. This is distinct from [Mr Minehead's Depth Charge](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Mr_Minehead%27s_Depth_Charge), which is accessed by speaking to Minehead in the Minefield area of Shimmerfin Deep and is a grid-based opponent/depth-charge game. **Uncertainty:** the Wiki does not identify which repository/game parameter name corresponds to the Button feature.

- **Cooking meal-bonus terminology:** [Cooking → Leveling Meals](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cooking#Leveling_Meals) says leveling meals provides cooking-speed bonuses through the [Diamond Chef bubble](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Alchemy/Kazam_Cauldron#Diamond_Chef) at meal level 11, the Fluoride atom at meal level 30, and Blood Marrow. [Cooking → Improving Meal Bonuses](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cooking#Improving_Meal_Bonuses) lists Black Diamond Rhinestone, shiny pets, Summoning battle wins, and ribbons as ways to strengthen meal bonuses. The Wiki's visible terms are “cooking speed,” “Meal and Fire Kitchen Speeds,” and “meal bonuses,” not a canonical `MealSpdz` label.

- **`MealSpdz`:** The official Wiki's [exact-term search returns no results](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=MealSpdz&format=json). **Unresolved:** `MealSpdz` may be an internal/save-data shorthand for meal cooking speed, but the Wiki alone does not establish that mapping or its formula semantics.

- **Crop Scientist:** [Farming → Scientist Blobulyte](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Farming#Scientist_Blobulyte) says the Jade Emporium unlocks this entity, which keeps a data sheet of all crops found and grants bonuses based on the crop count. [The Button](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/The_Button) uses the visible phrase “Crops, as shown by your Crop Scientist” in its requirements. **Uncertainty:** the Farming page's canonical heading is “Scientist Blobulyte,” while “Crop Scientist” is the visible shorthand; the Wiki does not expose the repository field/ID behind that wording.

- **Hole Bravery monument:** [The Caverns → Monument Of Bravery](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/The_Caverns#Monument_Of_Bravery) identifies it as the first Monument in the Shallow Caverns. Players accumulate AFK hours to show respect and unlock starting bonuses; each round uses swords against an enemy, with chest rewards. Its reward table explicitly includes multiplicative Cooking Speed and Bravery-specific bonuses. **Uncertainty:** the Wiki does not state that the repository's parameter name must be `Bravery` or identify a numeric monument ID.

- **Alchemy Bubble relevant to meals:** [Alchemy → Kazam Cauldron](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Alchemy/Kazam_Cauldron#Diamond_Chef) names the bubble **Diamond Chef** and describes it as making Meal and Fire Kitchen Speeds faster for every meal at level 11+. [Cooking](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cooking#Leveling_Meals) independently links that threshold to Diamond Chef. This supports the feature-name context only; it does not define any repository `MealSpdz` parameter.

- **Lab jewel / Mainframe:** [Laboratory](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Laboratory) defines the Mainframe as the World 4 system where characters are uploaded and connected to account-wide bonuses, and says jewels are additional nodes on the Mainframe board. The same page documents meal-related jewel names/effects: **Amethyst Rhinestone** makes meal cooking 1.5× (2.25× with the listed node effect), while **Black Diamond Rhinestone** increases displayed meal bonuses by 1.16× (1.24× with the listed node effect). **Unresolved:** the official Wiki does not identify a numeric “jewel 0” or “Mainframe 100” term, nor map either number to a named jewel, Lab level, or formula parameter. The [exact search for “Jewel 0” and Mainframe 100 returns no results](https://idleon.wiki/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=%22Jewel%200%22%20Mainframe%20100&format=json).

## Bottom line

The Wiki supports the names **The Button**, **Mr Minehead's Depth Charge**, **Diamond Chef**, **Scientist Blobulyte/Crop Scientist**, **Monument Of Bravery**, **Mainframe**, and named meal-related jewels. It does **not** resolve the internal labels `MealSpdz`, `jewel 0`, or `Mainframe 100`; those mappings must come from the repository's extraction/game evidence.
